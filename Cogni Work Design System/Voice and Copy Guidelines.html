THE LAST PASS

The 10-second checklist

Read every piece of copy once against this list before it ships. Cut what fails.

  • One claim per sentence
  • Numbers grounded, where present
  • Three registers used correctly
  • No emoji, no exclamations, no bingo words
  • One primary CTA
  • You / we / never I
  • Would a senior consultant nod, or wince?
